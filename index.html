Digital Legacy Archive

Architecture and technology plan for a digital archive of about fifty years of a scholar's work: structured data, a RESTful API and preservation practice. About

Regina Flores Mir is an AI consultant and educator whose career spans astrophysics, quantitative finance, and design and technology. She works with founders and corporate teams on data architecture and agentic AI systems, from strategy and workflow automation to full-stack builds. Her work has been incubated at The Met Media Lab, NYC Media Lab and the New Museum's NEW INC, and her MIT Media Lab work went to the 2016 Venice Biennale. She holds degrees from Columbia and Parsons, where she teaches Design and Technology. Her workshops make AI practical for non-technical teams, from executives to classrooms.
